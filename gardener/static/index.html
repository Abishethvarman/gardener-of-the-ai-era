Reading the calendar…

What's wrong with my plant?

Take the phone to the plant and photograph the leaf or stem that looks wrong. Gemma suggests possible causes and simple things to check. The photo goes only to your own model.

Ask about your calendar

Ask in your own words. Answers use only this place's sowing calendar, and the real dates are always shown underneath.

Is it time to sow this?

Photograph the front or back of a seed packet. Gemma reads it, and the calendar for your place decides whether to sow now.

My garden

Note what you planted. You'll see when each one should be ready, and when to sow another batch. Your list stays on this device.

What fits my balcony?

Tell us your pots and how many hours of direct sun they get. You'll get crops you can start now or soon.

Windows are rules of thumb from average season dates. This year's rain and your soil get the final say. Plant checks are suggestions from a small model, not a diagnosis.
